About Me

Hi, I’m Selina :) I am a Computer Science undergraduate along side with innovative design program and a strong interest in Human–Computer Interaction, where I enjoy combining technical development with human-centered design. Outside of work, I love music and dancing, traveling, and editing videos to document everyday moments. Exploring new places and experiences helps me observe how people interact with their surroundings and technology, often becoming a source of design inspiration. With a diverse background across engineering, design, and creative practice, I aim to design interactive systems that are thoughtful, intuitive, and grounded in real human needs.

Interest
